Background Remover

A color-based (chroma-key style) background remover — works best on flat, solid-color backgrounds. This is a heuristic, not AI segmentation, so it runs instantly and fully offline.

Runs 100% locally
Click the image to sample the background color (defaults to the top-left corner). Then adjust the tolerance until the background disappears cleanly. Works best when the subject stands out clearly from a solid or near-solid background — busy photo backgrounds won't cut out well.
