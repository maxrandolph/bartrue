PDF to Grayscale

Converts colored text, vector graphics and simple raster images to grayscale.

Runs 100% locally
Solid fills, strokes and photos (JPEG images) are desaturated. Patterns, gradients (shadings) and content inside nested Form XObjects are left as-is — a documented limitation of a from-scratch content-stream rewriter.
🔒 Everything happens in-memory in this tab; nothing is uploaded.
