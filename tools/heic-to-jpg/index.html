HEIC to JPG

Convert an iPhone HEIC photo to a widely-compatible JPG.

Runs 100% locally
HEIC decoding relies on your browser's built-in image support. Safari can usually open HEIC files directly; Chrome and Firefox often cannot, in which case this tool will report that the file could not be read.
🔒 Everything happens in-memory in this tab; nothing is uploaded.
